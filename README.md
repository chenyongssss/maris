# MARIS

**Memory-Aware Review & Insight Skill**

**Less output. More understanding. Better decisions.**

MARIS helps people understand, verify, and decide what matters in work performed by AI agents. It emphasizes evidence-linked review, user-approved cognitive profiles, and human decision checkpoints.

## Three demos

- **Coding** — real RevAgent history-memory implementation and retention-policy fix.
- **Research** — exact Poisson equation counterexample distinguishing finite samples from continuous unique continuation.
- **Decision** — transparent, assumption-sensitive comparison of open engineering software workflows.
