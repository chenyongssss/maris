# Baseline reference — illustrative, NOT model-run output

There are mature open-source foundations in all three directions: OMSimulator/OpenModelica for systems simulation, FreeCAD for parametric CAD, and OpenROAD for chip design. A startup should leverage these ecosystems rather than rebuild everything from scratch. Choose a direction based on team strengths and market demand.
